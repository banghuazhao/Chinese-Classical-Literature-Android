# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-04)

- Refresh the reader, catalog, navigation, and artwork for Android 16 and newer.
- Fix a crash when changing the reading background from a hidden Books tab.
- Add an age choice and privacy controls before requesting ads. Readers under 18 do not receive ads and cannot open age-restricted titles.
- Remove seven titles from the packaged catalog and their chapter and cover assets.
- Update release ad configuration, target Android SDK 37, and strengthen backup and release-build rules.
